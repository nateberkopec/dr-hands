Usage: dr-hands resume RUN_ID
Recover from durable local state and reconcile the original identity, generation,
deadline, resources, and authority. Serialize operations for the same run.
Do not blindly replay creates, regrant revoked authority, or extend the deadline.
If expired or removal started, continue removal only. A broken workflow definition
cannot prevent cleanup from the recorded ownership inventory.
No scheduler or daemon is required. A missing local record is not an empty cloud.
