Usage: dr-hands export RUN_ID
Explicitly copy declared outputs as opaque bytes to fixed local filenames.
Validate findings and verification outputs against their schemas without
rendering them. Prose and transcripts are for the operator; only agent-channel
finding fields feed a handoff.
Never render, execute, extract, or interpret guest-provided paths.
Derived outputs inherit private sensitivity if any readable image/input is private.
Export does not declassify data, feed a public run, extend expiry, or block removal.
Exports must finish before their runtime source disappears; removal does not wait.
